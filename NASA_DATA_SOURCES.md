# Official NASA Open Data & GIS Citations

**Project:** ECHOES OF SILENCE  
**Team:** Spaghetti Coders (Omar, Bashar, and Youssef)

Every coordinate, mission date, metric dimension, 3D mesh, and archival photograph in **Echoes of Silence** is sourced from official public-domain NASA repositories (`17 U.S.C. § 105`):

---

## 1. NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA)
- **URL:** [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov)
- **Role in Project:** Primary source for official spacecraft catalog IDs (`1969-059C`, `1969-059A-04`, `1972-096C`, `1967-035A`, `2020-052B`, `2003-032A`, `2003-027A`, `2018-042A`, `2007-034A`, `1996-068A`, `1996-008A`, `2007-043A`, `2009-011A`), launch/landing timestamps, dry masses, and true metric dimensions (`width × depth × height`).

## 2. NASA 3D Resources Portal
- **URL:** [https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov](https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov)
- **Role in Project:** Official public-domain glTF/GLB spacecraft 3D models and structural reference blueprints for the Apollo 11 Lunar Module, Ingenuity Mars Helicopter, InSight Lander, Viking/Phoenix Lander architecture, Dawn Ion Propulsion Probe, and Pioneer/Kepler spacecraft.

## 3. Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LRO / LROC)
- **URL:** [https://www.lroc.asu.edu](https://www.lroc.asu.edu)
- **Role in Project:** Verified selenographic coordinates and sub-meter Narrow Angle Camera (NAC) overhead reconnaissance imagery confirming the resting state of **Apollo 11 Eagle Descent Stage** (`0.6741° N, 23.4730° E`), **Apollo 11 LRRR** (`0.6734° N, 23.4729° E`), **Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle** (`20.1908° N, 30.7717° E`), and **Surveyor 3** (`3.0124° S, 23.4216° W`).

## 4. Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter — HiRISE Camera Archive
- **URL:** [https://www.uahirise.org](https://www.uahirise.org)
- **Role in Project:** Verified areocentric coordinates and `0.25 m/px` orbital imagery for **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter** (`18.4447° N, 77.4508° E`), **Opportunity Rover** (`1.9462° S, 354.4734° E`), **Spirit Rover** (`14.5684° S, 175.4726° E`), **InSight Lander** (`4.5024° N, 135.6234° E`), **Phoenix Lander** (`68.2188° N, 234.2508° E`), and **Sojourner Rover** (`19.13° N, 33.22° W`).

## 5. NASA Planetary Data System (PDS)
- **URL:** [https://pds.nasa.gov](https://pds.nasa.gov)
- **Role in Project:** Apollo Preliminary Science Reports (`SP-214`, `SP-235`, `SP-330`), InSight SEIS Marsquake catalog (`1,319` recorded seismic events), and Small Bodies Node telemetry for **NEAR Shoemaker** on Asteroid 433 Eros (`35.0° S, 324.0° E`) and **Dawn** in orbit around Dwarf Planet 1 Ceres (`24.0° N, 145.0° E`).

## 6. NASA Image and Video Library & NASA Exoplanet Archive
- **URL:** [https://images.nasa.gov](https://images.nasa.gov) · [https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu)
- **Role in Project:** Public-domain surface photography displayed in each spacecraft's Story Drawer and confirmed transit discovery telemetry (`2,662` exoplanets) for the **Kepler Space Telescope** (`1.05 AU` Earth-trailing heliocentric orbit).
