# AI & Synthetic Intelligence (SI) Transparency Disclosure

**Project:** ECHOES OF SILENCE  
**Challenge:** *Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars*  
**Team:** Spaghetti Coders (Omar, Bashar, and Youssef) — NASA Space Apps Challenge Cairo 2026

---

## 1. Independent Human Conception & Engineering
Team **Spaghetti Coders** independently conceived the curatorial architecture, three-tier spatial navigation system (Solar System Explorer, Orbital Museum Deck, and 1:1 Surface Stage), educational progression (`13/13 Explorer Passport`), and selection of the 13 historic NASA spacecraft.

## 2. How AI Tools Were Used
In strict compliance with the **NASA Space Apps Challenge Cairo 2026 Guidelines**, we disclose the following assistive uses of AI tools during the hackathon:

- **Google Antigravity & Google Gemini:** Used as an AI pair-programming assistant to scaffold React 19 / TypeScript components, tune Three.js (`r183`) PBR materials and custom GLSL atmospheric/solar shaders, and copyedit historical mission summaries into clear, age-appropriate first-person educational narratives for students aged 10–14.
- **Manus AI (`manus.ai`):** Used to structure the layout of our 7-slide presentation deck.

## 3. Scientific Integrity & Zero AI-Generated Imagery
- **100% Verified NASA Telemetry:** No scientific coordinates, mission dates, spacecraft dimensions, or light-delay calculations were invented by AI. Every data point is cross-referenced against official NASA repositories (`NSSDCA`, `LROC`, `MRO HiRISE`, `PDS`, `NASA 3D Resources`, and `NASA Image and Video Library`).
- **Zero AI-Generated Images or Logos:** No AI-generated images, synthetic photographs, or altered NASA insignia appear anywhere in the application, repository, or presentation materials. All screenshots depict our live working application and unmodified public-domain NASA archival imagery (`17 U.S.C. § 105`).
- **No NASA Endorsement Claimed:** This project is an independent educational submission for the NASA Space Apps Challenge and does not claim official NASA endorsement or sponsorship.
